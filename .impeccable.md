## Design Context

### Users
Russian B2B market, educational content professionals: course creators, online school owners, expert-entrepreneurs aged 30-55. View KP pages via direct link, during business hours, on laptop/desktop. Task: evaluate a proposal and decide whether to proceed.

### Brand Personality
EdAgency is a methodical, decisive, editorial agency. They do structured work: interview → methodology → content → production.
3 brand words: **disciplined, content-forward, reliable**

### Aesthetic Direction
Light theme — professional, readable, trust-building. Not a SaaS startup. Not dark "creative agency". More like a well-designed business document meets editorial design.

Anti-references: generic purple SaaS dark mode, AI gradient glows, rounded card grids with decorative icons, heavy shadows.

Colors: warm off-white background (#F7F5F0), near-black text (#1A1914), deep indigo accent (#2B3A9A — navy, authoritative, different from "startup purple").

Fonts: Raleway (display/headings — has authority without being corporate, works for Russian) + Barlow (body — humanist, readable, excellent for Russian text) + Barlow Condensed (labels/monospace substitute — technical feel without JetBrains Mono cliché).

### Design Principles
1. **Смыслы первее эстетики** — typography and spacing serve the text, not decorate it
2. **Никаких свечений** — no gradient glows, no blur effects, no decorative radial gradients
3. **Конкретность в деталях** — use numbers, specifics, real client names everywhere possible
4. **Доверие через порядок** — clean layout signals reliability better than visual tricks
5. **Тест заголовков** — if the heading-only scan tells the full story, design is working (Fobesko rule)
